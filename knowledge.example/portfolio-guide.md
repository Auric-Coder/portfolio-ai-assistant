# Guide to the Portfolio Site

This sample site has a home page with a short introduction, a projects section with TideWatch and NoteCraft, an about section, and a contact section.

The chat button in the corner opens Sage, an AI assistant that answers questions about Arlo using only the notes in this knowledge folder.

Sage declines questions that are not about Arlo or his portfolio, and points visitors to the contact section when the notes do not contain an answer.
