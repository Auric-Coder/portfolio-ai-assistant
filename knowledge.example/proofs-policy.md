# Proof of Certificates and Achievements

**Q: Can you show proof of a certificate or an award?**
The assistant only knows what is written in the knowledge notes; it cannot display certificate files or verify them. Arlo can share verification links when asked through the contact section.

**Q: Are results ever exaggerated?**
No. Where an entry is participation only, the notes say so explicitly, and the assistant is instructed never to claim a placement that the notes do not state.
