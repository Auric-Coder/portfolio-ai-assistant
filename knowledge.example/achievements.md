# Achievements and Competitions

## Harrowgate Data Dash 2025 (fictional), second place

In November 2025 Arlo's team of three placed second out of 41 teams at the Harrowgate Data Dash, a campus data hackathon. Their entry was an early version of TideWatch.

## Participation only

Arlo also took part in the Millbrook Math Circle league in 2022. It was participation only: there is no placement and no result is claimed.
